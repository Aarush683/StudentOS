What is StudentOS?

StudentOS is a student-focused academic and career management platform designed to bring important student activities into one organized system.

Instead of managing attendance, assignments, exams, internships, resources and career information separately, StudentOS provides a single dashboard to manage and track them.

Why StudentOS?

Students often use different platforms, spreadsheets and notes to keep track of their academic and career activities. StudentOS aims to make this process simpler by bringing everything together in one place.

  • Track academic performance
  • Monitor attendance
  • Manage assignments
  • Plan and track examinations
  • Track internship applications
  • Organize learning resources
  • Maintain a professional profile
  • Build and manage a resume

Key Features

Feature Purpose
Dashboard Provides an overview of important academic and career information.
Subjects Tracks subjects, marks, grades, credits and attendance.
Attendance Helps students monitor attendance and required attendance levels.
Assignments Organizes assignments, deadlines, priorities and completion status.
Exams Manages examination schedules and study planning.
Internships Tracks internship applications, deadlines and application status.
Resume Stores career information, projects, skills and certifications.
Resources Organizes academic and career learning resources.
Profile Maintains student information and career interests.
